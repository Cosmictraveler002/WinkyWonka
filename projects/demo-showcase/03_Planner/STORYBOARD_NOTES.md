## 🎬 Creative Direction

- **Core Idea:** High-impact authored visual narrative balancing kinetic typography, deep atmospheric lighting, and high-fidelity product reveals built around Template B (Burst -> Hold -> Burst -> Payoff).
- **Visual Language:** Grounded editorial dark mode aesthetic, avoiding generic AI-slop tropes. High contrast between crisp foreground typographic marks and subtle volumetric particle fields.
- **Camera Language:** Deliberate, cinematic focal shifts with subtle continuous drift. Fast snaps on rhythmic accents, stable holds on key typography and payoff.
- **Editing Language:** Non-uniform temporal cutting following Template B (Burst -> Hold -> Burst -> Payoff). Opening burst (2.5s) -> fast technical escalation (1.8s) -> extended payoff hold (3.0s). Cuts are motivated by typographic reveals rather than arbitrary beats.
- **Performance Language:** Zero mechanical float. Elements settle with organic spring physics (damping: 15, stiffness: 220), establishing weight and tactile authority.
- **Ending Strategy:** Decisive focal settlement on brand call-to-action with glowing accent pulse, holding until visual dissolve.
